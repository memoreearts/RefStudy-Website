Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-13

The short version

RefStudy works on your own device. Your reference photos, drawings and projects are not uploaded to us. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. The only thing we hold about you is your email address, and only if you join the launch list.

This website

If you join the launch list, your email address is stored by our email provider, Kit, so we can email you about RefStudy. You can unsubscribe from any email at any time, and you can ask us to delete your address.

This site doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages and its fonts come from Google Fonts, so, as with any website, those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

The app

This covers the RefStudy app on Android and the web app at app.refstudy.app.

  • Photos and projects. Images you open and projects you save stay on your device. All image processing happens on your device. On Android, projects are saved in the app's own storage; in the web app, saving a project downloads it to your computer.
  • Work in progress. So you don't lose your work, the picture you have open may be kept in local storage on your device (in the app on Android, in your browser for the web app) so it can come back if the app closes or the page reloads. Going back to the home screen clears it. Your settings, such as your paint palette and tool preferences, are stored on your device too.
  • Camera. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo, for example of your drawing in Check my drawing. Those images stay on your device.
  • Sharing. If you use Share, your device's own share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes.
  • The web app's hosting. The web app is delivered by Google Firebase Hosting, which receives standard technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. The app itself doesn't send anything back.

What we don't do

No account or sign-in, no advertising or advertising ID, and no analytics or crash-reporting services in the app. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services named above, which help run RefStudy.

Children

RefStudy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@refstudy.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a future version adds something like accounts, syncing between devices or purchases, we'll update this page before that version is released.
